# Level 3: The Free Lunch

This vault charges a 1% fee on every withdrawal. The fee goes to the protocol's
treasury and the rest goes to the destination the owner chooses.

The access checks are sound this time. Even so, one owner has emptied a large
vault and the treasury received nothing.

**Your goal:** make sure every withdrawal pays its fee, without changing what a
normal withdrawal pays. Edit the program, then submit. Hidden tests will attack
your fix.
